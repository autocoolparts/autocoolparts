About AutoCoolParts

Empowering Canadian Outdoor Vehicle Owners Since 2024

Our Story

AutoCoolParts was founded with a simple mission: to provide Canadian truckers, RV enthusiasts, and outdoor vehicle owners with access to premium electric air conditioning technology that's built for our unique climate.

After years of seeing outdoor vehicle owners rely on outdated cooling systems or expensive diesel generators, we partnered with leading engineers to develop AC units specifically designed for the Canadian market — from the harsh winters of Northern Ontario to the hot summers of British Columbia.

Today, we're proud to serve customers across all provinces and territories, delivering reliable, eco-friendly cooling solutions that keep adventures going year-round.

Our Mission & Values

🌱 Environmental Stewardship
